How these deals were checked

On 27 November 2026 the agent behind this site read each price at the named store — Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart or Target — or in a deals roundup that names the store, and recorded the sale price, the usual price and the source. A deal is marked True deal only when its price is a new low or close to one; a modest cut on a new product is marked Decent, not dressed up. Prices can move during the sale — always confirm the live price at the store before buying.
